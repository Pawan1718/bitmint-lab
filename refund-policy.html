Refund Policy

This policy explains the general refund and cancellation principles applicable to Bitmint Lab software subscriptions and custom development services.

Last updated: July 18, 2026

1. General Policy

Refund eligibility depends on the type of product or service, work already performed, resources committed and the specific commercial terms agreed with the customer.

Where a signed agreement, proposal, quotation, subscription plan or statement of work contains specific refund or cancellation terms, those terms will take precedence.

2. SaaS and Software Subscriptions

Subscription fees generally cover access to software, infrastructure and related services for the purchased billing period.

Fees already charged for an active subscription period are generally non-refundable unless:

  • A refund is specifically required under applicable law.
  • An incorrect or duplicate charge has occurred.
  • Bitmint Lab explicitly approves a refund after reviewing the circumstances.

3. Subscription Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation according to the applicable subscription or service terms.

Cancellation normally prevents future renewal or billing and does not automatically create an entitlement to a refund for an already active or completed billing period.

4. Custom Software Development

Custom software projects involve dedicated planning, development time, design work, engineering resources and project-specific commitments.

Payments relating to work already started, completed milestones or resources already committed are generally non-refundable.

Any refund or settlement for a cancelled custom project will be determined according to the applicable proposal, quotation, milestone plan, statement of work or agreement.

5. Advance and Initial Payments

Advance, booking, initiation or token payments may be used to reserve development capacity, begin analysis, planning, design, setup or implementation.

Such amounts may become partially or fully non-refundable once work has started or resources have been allocated, subject to the applicable project agreement.

6. Completed Milestones

Payments made for completed, delivered or approved milestones are generally non-refundable.

Issues relating to defects or agreed requirements will normally be handled according to the correction, warranty or support terms applicable to the project rather than through an automatic refund.

7. Third-Party Expenses

Charges paid or committed to third-party providers are generally non-refundable once incurred.

This may include:

  • Domain registrations.
  • Cloud hosting or server charges.
  • Third-party software licenses.
  • API or communication service charges.
  • App store or platform fees.
  • Other external services purchased for a project.

8. Duplicate or Incorrect Payments

If you believe you were charged twice or charged an incorrect amount, contact Bitmint Lab with the relevant transaction details.

Verified duplicate or incorrect payments will be reviewed and corrected or refunded as appropriate.

9. Refund Requests

Refund requests should include sufficient information to identify the relevant transaction, subscription or project.

Send requests to:

contact@bitmintlab.in

Submission of a request does not automatically guarantee approval of a refund.

10. Approved Refunds

Where a refund is approved, it will generally be processed using an appropriate available payment method.

Actual settlement time may depend on banks, payment gateways and other financial service providers outside Bitmint Lab's direct control.

11. Policy Updates

Bitmint Lab may update this Refund Policy as our products, services and commercial models evolve.

The latest applicable version will be published on this page.
