WhatsApp campaigns and templates: a practical overview

Campaigns and reusable templates make business messaging more organized, but thoughtful planning and customer consent matter just as much as the sending tools.

1. Start with the audience

Decide who should receive the update, why it matters, and whether the recipient has provided appropriate consent for that type of message.

2. Use a clear template

Good templates have a specific purpose, useful context and a straightforward next step. Keep them short enough to scan on a phone.

3. Review before scheduling

Check the campaign's audience, message category, variables, timing and any required approvals before sending.

4. Measure what matters

Delivery information and customer responses can help teams improve future communication. Avoid treating messages sent as a measure of success on its own.
